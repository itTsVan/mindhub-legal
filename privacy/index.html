Mind Hub Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Mind Hub is a notes, diary, journal and projects app with an AI chat. This policy explains what data the app and its server handle, why, who else receives it, and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible

Mind Hub is developed by Ivan Tsukylo, a sole proprietor (FOP) registered in Ukraine (“we”), who is the controller of your personal data. For any question about your data, or to use any of your rights below, write to support@mymindhub.app.

What stays on your device

Your notes, diary pages, journal, projects, tables, drawings, voice recordings, profile picture and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them unless you send them to the AI chat yourself (see below). Events from your phone calendar are read on the device to show them beside your notes; they never leave the device.

Because we do not keep a copy, this data cannot be recovered if you delete the app or lose or replace your phone. Use Backup in the app to export a backup file (.zip) and keep it somewhere safe. The backup file contains your notes, media and settings unencrypted; wherever you save it (Files, iCloud Drive, a computer, email) is under your control, not ours.

On iPhone or iPad, the app’s data on the device may also be included in your iCloud or computer (Finder / iTunes) backups of the device, under Apple’s terms and your backup settings. On Android, the app opts out of the system backup.

What we collect

Data Why
Account: email address, nickname, password (stored only as a hash), or the account identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google To create your account, sign you in, send verification and password reset codes, and keep your chats and plan tied to you.
AI chat: your messages, the notes, projects and tables you attach, the diary, journal or calendar entries you allow the chat to read, and the images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF) and documents (PDF, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, Word, Excel, PowerPoint) you attach. The chat does not accept audio or video files. To answer you and to show your chat history on your devices. Chat text is stored encrypted on our server; files are stored in Cloudflare R2.
Reports: when you report an AI reply, the reason and comment you give, the text of that reply, your previous message in the chat, and your account, chat and message identifiers To review the reply and act on it (moderation). The report is emailed to our support address (see “Who else receives your data”).
Usage: how much of the AI allowance you have used To apply the limits of your plan.
Purchases: your plan, subscription status and purchase history To unlock the features you paid for and restore them on another device.
Diagnostics: crash reports, device model, operating system and app version To find and fix crashes.
Analytics: which features are used, as counts and identifiers, together with an app instance identifier, and your approximate location (country, region or city), which Firebase and AppsFlyer derive from your IP address To understand which parts of the app are useful and, through AppsFlyer, to attribute installs and measure our advertising (which campaigns or sources bring new users). Approximate location is used for both. Analytics never contain the text of your notes or messages, and never your precise location.
Security: your IP address and the app’s User-Agent (app and system version) in our server’s request logs and sign-in sessions; on iPhone or iPad, an Apple DeviceCheck token To keep the service secure, limit repeated requests and abuse, protect your sign-in sessions, and allow only one free AI account per device. Apple stores two bits for the device, not your data.

Diagnostics, analytics and install attribution are collected only after you allow them when the app asks, and are linked to your account identifier. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share analytics and crash reports. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

Who else receives your data

  • Anthropic runs the Claude model that writes the AI chat’s replies. It receives what you send in the chat, as listed above, only after you allow it in the app. Of the files you attach, it receives the content of images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF), PDF and text files (plain text, Markdown, CSV, JSON). Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and images in other formats, are not sent to the model: it receives only their file name, type and size. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms it does not use this content to train its models, and it deletes it within 30 days, unless it is flagged for a breach of Anthropic’s usage policy (then kept for up to 2 years) or the law requires longer. You can withdraw this in Settings → Privacy; the chat stops working until you allow it again.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage) keeps the files you attach in the chat.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) receives diagnostics and analytics, only while you allow them, and derives your approximate location from your IP address.
  • AppsFlyer receives an install identifier and basic app activity (installs, sessions, and purchase events forwarded by RevenueCat), with your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, to attribute installs and measure our advertising (where installs come from). It does not receive the advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID) or any of your content, and is used only while you allow analytics.
  • RevenueCat, together with Apple App Store or Google Play, processes subscriptions. We never see your payment details. While you allow analytics, RevenueCat also receives device identifiers (such as the iOS identifier for vendor) and your IP address to link purchases to installs.
  • Apple and Google confirm your identity when you use Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Dictation in the chat uses the speech recognition of your phone. When the phone cannot recognise speech on the device, the audio is sent to Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) for recognition under their terms. The audio never reaches our server.
  • Resend sends verification and password reset codes to your email address. Our hosting provider runs the server.
  • Reports of AI replies are sent by email: Resend sends the message, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards our support address to a Google Gmail inbox, and it is read there by us. Google stores the email in that inbox.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, in particular in the United States (Anthropic, Google Firebase, AppsFlyer, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, Resend, Google Gmail). When data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland goes to a country without an adequacy decision, the transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (and the UK addendum) or, where the provider is certified, on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for details of the safeguard used for a provider.

Legal basis

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Contract — to provide your account, the AI chat, your plan and subscriptions, and to send sign-in codes.
  • Consent — for sending your chat content to Anthropic, and for analytics, install attribution and crash reports. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy; this does not affect what was processed before.
  • Legitimate interest — to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and enforce plan limits (server logs, request limits, the one-free-account-per-device check).

How long we keep it

We keep your account, chats and files until you delete them or your account. Deleting a chat in the app removes it from our server. Report emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the report and then deleted, and no longer than 12 months; they are not deleted when you delete the chat or your account. Server request logs are deleted automatically within 90 days; request-limit counters expire within their time window; a sign-in session’s record is removed after the session expires or is ended. Crash reports and analytics are kept by Firebase for a limited period under its own retention settings.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, all chats, messages, attached files, reports and subscription records from our server, and the data stored on that device. An active subscription is managed by Apple or Google — cancel it in your store settings so you are not charged again.

Some data is kept for a limited time after that:

  • AI usage: how much of the AI allowance the account used in the last 7 days is kept for 7–8 days, then deleted automatically. It is stored under a one-way hash of your verified email address and your Apple or Google account identifier (not the address itself), together with the former account number, only so that deleting and re-creating an account does not reset the plan limits (abuse prevention).
  • RevenueCat: when you delete your account we ask RevenueCat to delete your customer record and its attributes. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their terms.
  • Sign in with Apple: when you delete an account that uses Sign in with Apple in the app on an iPhone or iPad, confirming with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the app’s access to your Apple ID. If you delete it another way (for example with an emailed code on Android), access is not revoked automatically: remove Mind Hub in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple) yourself.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) and AppsFlyer: data they already received is not deleted when you delete your account. It is kept under each provider’s own retention period and then deleted automatically. You can ask us to request its deletion earlier.
  • Anthropic: as described above, within 30 days unless flagged or required by law.
  • Server request logs and report emails: within the periods in “How long we keep it”.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to restrict or object to its use, and you can withdraw consent at any time — in Settings → Privacy, or by writing to us. If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. Write to support@mymindhub.app.

Children

Mind Hub is intended only for people aged 18 and older and is rated 18+ in the app stores. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Your data, your device

The short version of our Privacy Policy.

  • Stored on your deviceNotes, diary, journal, projects and tables live on your device. Use Backup to keep a copy. Your AI chat history is also stored on our server.
  • AI only with your permissionThe chat sends what you write or attach to our server and to Anthropic’s Claude model — only after you allow it. You can withdraw it in Settings → Privacy.
  • No ads, no sellingWe don’t show ads, sell your data or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Free and paid plans

Mind Hub is free to use, including the AI chat with a usage allowance. Plus, Pro and Max are auto-renewable subscriptions that raise the AI allowance and lift the limit on project notes; Pro and Max also allow more projects and tables. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

  • Manage or cancelOn iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
  • Restore purchasesOn a new device, sign in and tap Restore purchases on the Subscription screen.
  • TermsUsing Mind Hub means accepting our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Support

Answers to common questions. Can’t find yours? Write to us.

How do I move my notes to a new device?

Open Account → Backup and export a backup file. On the new device, sign in and restore it from the same screen.

Why does the AI chat ask for permission?

To answer, the chat sends your messages and attachments to our server and to Anthropic. We ask before anything is sent, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy.

I reached the AI limit. What now?

The allowance refreshes over time. Paid plans have a larger allowance.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot your password?”, enter your email and the code we send you, then choose a new password.

How do I delete my account?

In the app open Account → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account, chats, messages, attached files, usage and subscription records from our server, and the data stored on that device. Can’t open the app? Email support@mymindhub.app from your account’s address. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in your App Store subscriptions.
